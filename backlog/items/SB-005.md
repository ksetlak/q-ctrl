## Description

In actuality the cards render properly, but the area on which they render is a small scrollable rectangle smaller v3ertically than even a single card, that's why only a (fraction of) a single card is visible. This card container area occupies just a tiny fraction of a column's total height, while it should dynamically adjust to span the entire column vertically - always.
