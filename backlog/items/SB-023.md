## Description

When a card is picked up and dropped at or near its original position — including directly on itself, just above it, or just below it — the card is moved to the top of the column instead of staying in place. The expected behaviour is that the card returns to its original position (effectively a no-op).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Dropping a card on its own slot (original position) leaves the column order unchanged.
- [ ] Dropping a card immediately above its original position leaves the column order unchanged.
- [ ] Dropping a card immediately below its original position leaves the column order unchanged.
- [ ] Dropping a card further away from its original position still reorders correctly.

## Notes

Root cause is likely in `reorder_ticket` or `order_for_drop` in `ticket.rs`: when `after_id` resolves to the dragged card itself (or the card just above it), the computed order may place it before all other cards. A guard comparing the resulting position to the original position and skipping the write when unchanged would fix this.

Same-column drops are the primary case; cross-column drops near the top of the target column may exhibit similar behaviour.
